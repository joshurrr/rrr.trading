# Asset Intelligence modal — 16 October 2026

Frontend presentation only. The shared native dialog retains its existing dark/neon design, asset triggers, bounded GET requests, cancellation, retry, close animation, focus return and body-scroll restoration. No backend, strategy, decision, risk, order, leverage or position-management changes.

The permanent header shows asset identity, Bybit perpetual price in USDT, 24h change, verified paper mode, central v2 position status, latest new-entry decision and the selected V2 decision direction with its horizon. A missing overall foundation aggregate is not presented as missing active V2 intelligence. Quotes require a positive price and snapshot time within two minutes, at most 30 seconds in the future, with available market evidence. The existing separate USD market-summary quote remains in Advanced. Older Bybit snapshots are explicitly stale/unverified rather than presented as current quotes.

Five keyboard-accessible tabs provide Overview, Trades, Intelligence, News & Market and Advanced. Desktop width is capped at 1180px. Header/tabs remain above one scrolling content region; mobile tabs wrap. Cards inside the persistent radio shell reuse its parent dialog, preserving the originating card and avoiding the short iframe viewport on phones. Arrow keys, Home and End switch tabs. Overview explains saved conditions and entry status. Intelligence reads the active `decision.timeframe_assessments` for 15m/1h/4h, with backend direction, opportunity score (0–100), confidence and state. Foundation `overall_state` / `timeframes` are retained only in Advanced and are not reactivated. No overall aggregate or inferred agreement is calculated in the browser. Freshness expires while the dialog remains open.

Trades reads the existing BotRegistry reporting endpoints and reuses its current-run and native-fill acceptance. Central reports must match the execution run; Slingshot retains its separate bot/mode/run checks and is never merged into the central run. Any additional generation guard still applies. Missing or mismatched reports never prove absence of a position. Current reporting expires after 45 seconds; reopening refreshes it. Expired open observations lose their current P&L and status, while closed trades and historical entry evidence remain visible. P&L is native reported data, colored green/red, with realized and unrealized labels. Entry/exit, recorded leverage, stake and exit reason are shown where available. Closed-trade missing leverage and missing original rationale explicitly show Historical evidence unavailable. A saved execution rationale is joined only by matching run, bot and exact v2 opportunity/sizing entry tag. Original decision, sizing and authorization IDs must also match before their evidence is displayed; the latest new-entry reason never substitutes for it. An unmatched latest execution remains labeled saved evidence, including recorded close price and realized P&L when its existing close payload provides them. Live positions are not verified by these paper feeds.

News initially shows three returned asset-specific articles ranked by recorded relevance, then publication time; Show more/fewer expands the rest. Safe HTTPS links and text-node rendering remain. Collection health and unknown event timing remain visible. Advanced retains the existing evidence sections, selection evidence, versions, IDs and expandable full public API/native-reporting evidence. Not applicable, Stale, Not calculated, Not collected, Not recorded and Historical evidence unavailable distinguish known missing-data situations; failed current verification remains explicit.

Data repair findings:

- Foundation `overall_state` and `timeframes` are intentional placeholders; active V2 assessments already exist under `decision.timeframe_assessments`. The UI mapping is repaired without inventing an overall aggregate or reviving foundation workers.
- Original decision/sizing/authorization payloads already exist on the latest execution and now have a readable historical disclosure. Existing GET `/api/v2/intelligence/execution/records` recovers older rationale and risk adjustments by exact run/entry IDs. Its 50 current-run summaries do not expose full older authorization payloads; unavailable details are not reconstructed from subsequent proposals or counterfactuals.
- Classification works, including signed directions and intentionally UNKNOWN price/prediction stories. Collection failures can withhold otherwise calculated signals; available primary searches can still fail the existing conservative coverage policy when another mapped-story source fails. Public diagnostics report `source_fetch_or_parse_failed` without distinguishing transport from parsing. No collector or classifier change is made.
- High relevance is an identity-match score: conversion pages and incidental mentions can score highly without supplying a signed catalyst. Changing relevance, source-coverage requirements, weights, cadence or safety deadlines could affect decisions and requires separate approval.
- Real stale decisions include expired positions/market/history evidence; dormant assets outside the scoring pool are not continually reevaluated. The UI names recorded expiry causes, honors deadlines while open (including Advanced), and distinguishes future/unverified data from ordinary expiry. Closed trade history does not expire with current reporting.

The focused modal suite adds V2/foundation distinction, selected/no-selected horizons, signed/unknown/degraded news and tiny signal precision, historical proposal/authorization/fill separation, exact original ID/run checks, older summaries and expiry. No backend deployment, migration, bot restart or backend regression suite was needed. Live verification remains separate from local fixtures.
