# Asset Intelligence modal — 16 October 2026

Frontend presentation only. The shared native dialog retains its existing dark/neon design, asset triggers, bounded GET requests, cancellation, retry, close animation, focus return and body-scroll restoration. No backend, strategy, decision, risk, order, leverage or position-management changes.

The permanent header shows asset identity, Bybit perpetual price in USDT, 24h change, verified paper mode, central v2 position status, latest new-entry decision and supported overall assessment. Quotes require a positive price and snapshot time within two minutes, at most 30 seconds in the future, with available market evidence. The existing separate USD market-summary quote remains in Advanced. Older Bybit snapshots are explicitly stale/unverified rather than presented as current quotes.

Five keyboard-accessible tabs provide Overview, Trades, Intelligence, News & Market and Advanced. Desktop width is capped at 1180px. Header/tabs remain above one scrolling content region; mobile tabs wrap. Cards inside the persistent radio shell reuse its parent dialog, preserving the originating card and avoiding the short iframe viewport on phones. Arrow keys, Home and End switch tabs. Overview explains saved conditions and entry status. Intelligence compares independent 15m/1h/4h assessments, withholding stale directions/scores/confidence. It does not replace those with decision-engine scores. Freshness expires while the dialog remains open.

Trades reads the existing BotRegistry reporting endpoints and reuses its current-run and native-fill acceptance. Central reports must match the execution run; Slingshot retains its separate bot/mode/run checks and is never merged into the central run. Any additional generation guard still applies. Missing or mismatched reports never prove absence of a position. Reporting expires after 45 seconds; reopening refreshes it. P&L is native reported data, colored green/red, with realized and unrealized labels. Entry/exit, recorded leverage, stake and exit reason are shown where available. Closed-trade missing leverage and missing original rationale explicitly show Historical evidence unavailable. A saved execution rationale is joined only by the exact v2 opportunity/sizing entry tag; the latest new-entry reason never substitutes for it. An unmatched latest execution remains labeled saved evidence, including recorded close price and realized P&L when its existing close payload provides them. Live positions are not verified by these paper feeds.

News initially shows three returned asset-specific articles ranked by recorded relevance, then publication time; Show more/fewer expands the rest. Safe HTTPS links and text-node rendering remain. Collection health and unknown event timing remain visible. Advanced retains the existing evidence sections, selection evidence, versions, IDs and expandable full public API/native-reporting evidence. Not applicable, Stale, Not calculated, Not collected, Not recorded and Historical evidence unavailable distinguish known missing-data situations; failed current verification remains explicit.
