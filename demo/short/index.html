Looks for a pullback within a trend confirmed by closed 1-hour candles. On the 15-minute chart, price must cross back above the 20-period exponential moving average (EMA) with rising RSI for a long, or below it with falling RSI for a short. The 20/50/200 EMAs, MACD and non-zero volume must also agree. Exits use weakening signals, profit targets, a stop-loss and a trailing stop.

Macro & news · research only. Daily FRED macro reports and monitored news feed separate shadow analysis, which evaluates context alongside strategy signals without changing trades. These inputs currently do not change this bot's entries, exits or position sizes. A structured economic event calendar is not connected; missing or stale research remains unavailable.
