Steps 1–3 · Select an hourly candidate

Bot strategy · hourly trends with 4-hour short confirmation

The configured rules sit beside each stage’s public observations. One hour is the analysis interval; a trade opens only when a candidate passes the remaining checks.

1 · Market Scan

The bot scans its configured Bybit USDT futures pairs using closed hourly candles. It can trade long or short, with isolated margin and fixed 1× leverage.

Stable EMA calculations need 1,000 candles. Missing history prevents a valid signal. The page refreshes every 15 seconds; that refresh does not create a new trading signal.

2 · Technical Signal

Hourly trend, price, RSI and MACD must agree on the same closed hourly candle before the signal moves to confirmation.

Exact long and short rules
  • Long: EMA20 > EMA50 > EMA200; close > EMA50; 52 < RSI < 70; MACD > its signal line.

3 · 4-hour Short Confirmation

Long trades follow the hourly uptrend; short trades also need a confirmed 4-hour downtrend. The hourly strategy does not require 4-hour uptrend confirmation for longs.

Before context is checked, the entry must still match the latest closed hourly signal, be no more than 75 minutes after that candle closes, and be within 0.5 ATR of its closing price. ATR uses 14 periods to measure recent price range.

Closed-candle confirmation
  • Short: the last available closed 4-hour candle must have EMA20 < EMA50 < EMA200 and close < EMA50.
  • The confirming candle must already have closed at the hourly decision time, be less than one 4-hour interval old and have enough history for stable EMAs. Missing or stale confirmation blocks shorts.
  • Both directions must still match the latest closed hourly entry signal when an entry is considered.

Steps 4–6 · Context, risk and admission

Check the wider market before opening a position

The deployed hourly paper guard uses saved homepage evidence. Required data failures block new entries; existing positions continue their normal exit management.

4 · Macro, Market & Event Guard

The saved daily FRED report, crypto and asset context, relevant themes, verified news and available upcoming events check for opposing risk. Missing, stale, invalid or insufficient required data blocks new entries.

Macro, news and calendar rules
  • Macro: score ≤25 blocks longs; score ≥75 blocks shorts. Between these limits, other checks still apply. A usable saved report is required, with the report day changing at 07:00 Brisbane.
  • Market: usable crypto and traded-asset context are required, plus a BTC anchor for altcoins. Each required layer needs at least 50% data coverage and must be within its validity period. Existing directional context and theme rules can block a conflicting setup.
  • News: fresh, relevant, verified critical news from healthy trusted sources can veto entries. Contradicted, expired or attention-only headlines do not create a new veto.
  • Events: eligible, precisely timed, high-impact macro releases from the Fed, BLS or BEA pause entries from 60 minutes before to 30 minutes after the event. The saved calendar revision and source checks must match; tentative dates do not create a blackout.
  • Calendar coverage is partial. A complete economic calendar remains unavailable. Missing optional news or calendar coverage is recorded; it does not invent a blackout.

Data coverage and source confidence are separate from the macro score and trade success. This card does not publish the separate hourly guard’s individual votes. Daily macro report and sources.

Exit monitoring

Once a position opens, the original trend, momentum, profit-target and stop rules manage it. Missing entry context does not prevent normal exits.

Exact exit and protection rules
  • Long exit: close < EMA50 with RSI <48, or MACD < its signal line with RSI <50. Short exit: close > EMA50 with RSI >52, or MACD > its signal line with RSI >50. Candle exit signals require non-zero volume and can close losing trades.
  • Minimum profit targets: 6% initially, 3.5% after 6 hours, 2% after 12 hours and 1% after 24 hours from opening. These are elapsed hours, not a fixed holding deadline.
  • Stops: configured stop-loss −6%. Trailing activates after the 3.5% profit offset and follows the best price at a 2% distance. ATR restricts chased entries; it does not replace these stops.
  • Verified critical risk: positions opened under the new hourly guard can also receive a critical-news exit recommendation. Positions opened before the guard retain their original exits.
  • Runtime targets and stops operate separately from closed-candle signals. The public feed does not expose every exit check, guard recommendation or fill.
