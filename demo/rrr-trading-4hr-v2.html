How the 4hr bot makes decisions

Macro decision making · Test Bot entry filter

Technical signals propose new trades, then the saved daily FRED macro score (0–100) checks direction: 25 or lower can block new longs; 75 or higher can block new shorts. Between these limits, there is no extra macro block. Other entry checks and verified event risk checks still apply. Missing, stale or insufficient macro data lets the technical strategy continue. The macro filter applies to new entries only; exits and trade sizes are unchanged. General headlines remain research flags, and the economic event calendar is not connected. View the daily macro report and sources.

Bot strategy · 4-hour trends with daily confirmation

Follows 4-hour trends only when closed daily candles confirm the same direction. The 20, 50 and 200-period exponential moving averages (EMAs) check trend and price position; RSI and MACD check momentum, with non-zero volume required. Both long and short trades need this alignment. Exits use weakening trend or momentum, profit targets, a stop-loss and a trailing stop.

The live cards below show the retained evidence for a candidate entry. PASS means that stage passed; FAIL-OPEN means the bot was allowed to continue after a gate failure. UNKNOWN means the check or outcome is not recorded. A gate approval alone does not confirm that a trade opened.
