Exit monitoring

Once a position opens, the 15-minute strategy manages exits independently of new-entry checks. A trade can close through a weakening candle signal, its stop-loss, a profit target or its trailing stop.

Exact exit and protection rules
  • Candle exit signal: uses the last closed 15-minute candle with non-zero volume. Weakening EMA, RSI or MACD conditions can request an exit, including for a losing trade.
  • Stops and targets: the configured stop-loss, age-based profit target and trailing stop are monitored separately from the closed-candle signal.
  • Observed exit conditions are not proof that an order was submitted or filled. The public feed may not expose every current check.
