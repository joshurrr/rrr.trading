## Macro presentation

- Fetch saved daily macro reports through the existing public API; do not embed sample values as current readings.
- FRED is the primary macro source. Do not scrape TradingView.

- Keep source confidence, data coverage, macro score, and future trading weight distinct.
- `macro_events` remains unavailable until a reliable structured calendar source is connected.

### Phase 6 — Position Sizing & Portfolio Risk

- Shadow sizing only. No order, admission, gate, routing, bot stake/configuration, exit, execution, statistics or learning changes. Stop after Phase 6.
- Formula: configured base stake × bounded confidence × volatility × liquidity × portfolio × crypto-beta concentration × directional concentration × horizon multipliers, followed by hard stake/notional/exposure caps and downward cent rounding. Final notional = final stake × independently capped leverage. Units are USDT, not converted USD.
- Base stake is copied from the selected bot's read-only configuration, with a separate optional shadow override. The 7 October inspection found 1,000 USDT and five slots in all three running paper bots, rather than the request's illustrative 500 USDT/four slots. Those execution settings are unchanged. Shadow leverage defaults to 1× and cannot exceed 2×; confidence never changes leverage.
- Confidence uses the actual Phase 5 minimum (.55 currently). At/above that minimum and below .73: .50×; [.73,.83): 1×; [.83,.91): 1.25×; [.91,1]: 1.50×. Continuous boundaries avoid gaps. Evidence confidence is uncalibrated and is not a probability.
- Volatility (% per closed 5m bar): <.1: 1.10×; [.1,.3): 1×; [.3,.5): .80×; [.5,1): .60×; [1,1.5): .40×; >=1.5 blocks. Liquidity HIGH/MODERATE/LOW multipliers are 1/.85/.60; missing evidence is unavailable, turnover <1m USDT or spread >20bps blocks.
- Cross-bot snapshots aggregate the three independent paper USDT wallets and every verified open position. Wallet equity uses the reported total_bot values; exposure is entry notional (recorded margin stake × recorded leverage), with no hedge netting or fabricated current marked exposure.
